Project overview

An editorial concept for turning complicated cloud-infrastructure topics into a readable, visually coherent publication. The sample spread combines an atmospheric cover with a data-led opening page.

The challenge

Technical material can be dense even for experienced readers. The challenge was to find an editorial rhythm that highlights major ideas and important numbers while giving details enough room to breathe.

Design approach

The design features large display typography, generous white space, recurring grid geometry, restrained line charts, and a small consistent color system. All example statistics are fictional and are labeled as illustrative.

What the concept includes

  • Full cover layout with publication branding
  • Inside spread with narrative hierarchy and sample chart
  • Reusable typography, whitespace, and annotation rules
